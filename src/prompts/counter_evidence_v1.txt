## Evaluation Task
Analyze the evidence using the 4 Counter-Evidence Strategies:
1. INVERSE QUERY: Did users praise this exact factor or stay retained despite this challenge?
2. SEGMENT DRILL-DOWN: Does this problem affect all investor segments equally, or is it confined to a specific cohort (e.g., students or variable income)?
3. TEMPORAL CHECK: Is this a persistent structural problem, or a transitory spike caused by a single incident/date?
4. FREQUENCY SANITY: Is this theme driven by a vocal minority (< 5% of churn) or does it represent a high-volume retention threat?
