---

## 1. Context & Research Objectives

Public feedback classification across 5 sources revealed three major operational and psychological friction points causing early SIP drop-off:
1. **Involuntary Mandate Churn (`HC-RC-01`):** Mandates failing silently, triggering automated cancellation after 3 misses without adequate investor recourse.
2. **Return Expectation Disappointment (`HC-RC-03`):** First-time investors expecting guaranteed linear compounding, colliding with 3–6 month flat returns.
3. **UX & Modification Friction (`HC-RC-02`):** Inability to easily skip or pause a single installment without permanently stopping the SIP.

**Research Goal:** Validate whether these hypotheses reflect lived investor experiences and determine what UX or automated interventions would have successfully retained them.

---

## 2. Participant Recruitment Criteria & Screening Screener

### Target Profile
- **Total Participants:** ≥ 5 verified first-time mutual fund investors
- **Tenure:** Started first SIP within the last 12 months
- **Cohort Mix:**
  - **Cohort A (Mandate Failure):** 2 participants whose SIP was cancelled after failed bank debits (focus on SBI, ICICI, HDFC).
  - **Cohort B (Return Disappointment):** 2 participants who stopped/paused their SIP in months 3–6 after viewing flat or negative returns.
  - **Cohort C (UX Friction):** 1–2 participants who attempted to pause/modify their SIP but faced interface or payment issues.

### Screener Questions
1. *"When did you start your first mutual fund SIP on any platform?"* (Must be within 3–12 months).
2. *"Is your SIP currently active, paused, or cancelled?"* (Looking for cancelled or paused).
3. *"If cancelled or paused, what happened right before the status changed?"* (Screen for bank debit failure, market volatility, or pause difficulty).

## 3. Core Interview Protocols & Ethical Guardrails

### Ethical Guardrails (MANDATORY)
- **Zero Investment Advice:** Never suggest specific funds, equity vs. debt allocations, or market timing strategies.
- **Zero Guilt or FOMO:** Never imply the investor made a mistake by stopping their SIP.
- **Strict Anonymity:** All interview recordings, notes, and quotes must be stripped of PII before logging into the AI Evidence Log.

### Standardized Question Guides by Hypothesis

#### Hypothesis 1: Automated Mandate Recovery (`HC-RC-01`)
1. Can you walk me through what happened when your SIP installment didn't go through as scheduled?
2. Did you receive any prior notification or alert from Groww or your bank before the payment was missed? How clear was it?
3. When the mandate failed or the SIP was cancelled after consecutive misses, what was your initial reaction?
4. Did you intend to stop investing, or were you hoping the app would retry automatically or give you an option to pay manually?
5. What specific assistance or automated grace period would have enabled you to keep your SIP active?
6. Did you attempt to reach customer support or look on Reddit/YouTube for a solution? What was that experience like?

#### Hypothesis 2: Return Expectation Mismatch (`HC-RC-03`)
1. When you started your SIP, what returns or outcomes were you expecting in your first 3 to 6 months?
2. Did you use an in-app SIP return calculator before investing? What numbers or graphs do you remember seeing?
3. How did you feel when you checked your portfolio after 3 to 5 months and saw flat or negative returns?
4. Did anyone (a colleague, friend, or YouTube video) influence your decision to pause or withdraw?
5. If the app had shown you a historical range of returns (best-case vs. sideways market) instead of a fixed 15%, how would that have changed your expectations?

#### Hypothesis 3: UX & Payment Modification Friction (`HC-RC-02`)
1. When you decided you needed a break from investing for a month, what steps did you take in the app?
2. Did you find an option to skip a single installment, or did you feel forced to cancel the entire SIP?
3. Have you ever experienced a situation where money was deducted from your bank but fund units were not allotted? How did the app handle that?
