- **Target Root Cause:** `RC-01`
- **Corpus Frequency:** 148 items (46.54% of corpus)
- **Counter-Evidence Strength:** **High**
- **Revised Confidence Level:** **Low**
- **Recommendation:** **Downgrade confidence or Pivot hypothesis**

#### 4-Strategy Adversarial Evaluation

| Strategy | Signal | Finding Summary |
|---|---|---|
| **Inverse Query** | `CONTRADICTION FOUND (Low)` | Found 1 items where investors actively praised the seamless experience, indicating the issue is not universally experienced across all users. |
| **Segment Drill-down** | `CONTRADICTION FOUND (High)` | Segment concentration is 100.0%. The issue is heavily skewed to specific cohorts rather than universal. |
| **Temporal Check** | `CONTRADICTION FOUND (High)` | Temporal spike detected: 146/150 items clustered on a single date, suggesting a transitory incident. |
| **Frequency Sanity** | `SUPPORTED (None)` | Theme represents substantial volume (46.5% of corpus), confirming high operational significance. |

#### Contradicting Feedback Quotes

> "Auto debit on 1st of every month without fail. Set it and forget it. Excellent disciplined investing experience."

#### Synthesis & PM Guidance
Strong contradicting evidence found across multiple strategies. The assumption that 'RC-01' uniquely drives this theme is challenged by positive counter-examples and cohort skew.
