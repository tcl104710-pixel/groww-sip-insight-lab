## 3. Key Decisions & Agreements

### Decision 1: Immediate UPI AutoPay Migration & 1-Tap Recovery Flow (`HC-RC-01`)
- **Agreement:** Engineering will prioritize UPI AutoPay 2.0 integration to replace legacy e-mandates that suffer from high failure rates on PSU banks.
- **Guardrail:** Pre-debit balance check must notify users 24 hours prior to debit date, allowing them to top up balance or initiate a manual retry before a strike is logged.

### Decision 2: Qualitative User Research on Top 3 Hypotheses (`HC-RC-01`, `HC-RC-03`, `HC-RC-02`)
- **Agreement:** Research team accepted the standardized Interview Validation Guides generated by the hypothesis engine.
- **Commitment:** Conduct a minimum of 5 qualitative in-depth user interviews across target cohorts (users with failed mandates, users pausing within 6 months).

### Decision 3: Deprecation of Single-Point Linear Return Projections (`HC-RC-03`)
- **Agreement:** Design will redesign the SIP calculator to show realistic range-based CAGR outcomes (e.g. 10th percentile, median, 90th percentile) with clear 3–5 year time horizon disclaimers.

### Decision 4: Audit & Ethics Compliance
- **Agreement:** Compliance confirmed that all generated cards and reports adhere to SEBI norms and internal ethics standards: zero stock recommendations, zero FOMO language, full PII scrubbing.
