CRITICAL DISAMBIGUATION RULES (v2 Iteration):
1. STATED INTENT VS ACTUAL ACTION:
   - "stated_intent": what the user expresses forward-looking willingness to do ('continue', 'pause', 'stop', 'neutral', 'unknown').
   - "actual_action": the current state of their SIP ('active', 'paused', 'cancelled', 'unknown').
   - Example 1: "I'm thinking of stopping my SIP next week" -> stated_intent="stop", actual_action="active".
   - Example 2: "My bank rejected the debit again so SIP was cancelled by Groww" -> stated_intent="unknown" or "continue", actual_action="cancelled".
   - Example 3: "Is it a good time to stop?" -> stated_intent="neutral", actual_action="active".

2. INVOLUNTARY LAPSE (ST-01 / RC-01) vs DELIBERATE EXIT (ST-02):
   - If SIP ended due to insufficient funds, bank server downtime, NACH mandate rejection, or the 3-strike auto-cancellation rule: MUST BE ST-01 and RC-01.
   - If user actively hit "Cancel SIP" or "Pause SIP" due to personal financial choice, dissatisfaction, or goal completion: MUST BE ST-02.
   - Continuing investors: stop_type is ST-03 (not_applicable).

3. RC-03 (RETURN DISAPPOINTMENT) vs RC-06 (KNOWLEDGE GAP):
   - RC-03: Investor expected 15% because calculator/finfluencer promised it, but got 7% (expectation vs reality gap).
   - RC-06: Investor confuses market volatility with loss or fraud ("My fund went down 200 rs in 1 day, Groww stole my money!").

4. RETENTION FACTORS (RF-01 to RF-07):
   - For churned or cancelled investors, set retention_factor to null.
   - For active investors, identify what keeps them investing (e.g. RF-06 for automated SIP, RF-01 for positive returns, RF-07 for understanding rupee-cost averaging).

5. CONFIDENCE SCORING GUIDELINE:
   - 0.90–1.00: Explicit statement directly matches taxonomy definitions.
   - 0.75–0.89: Clear inference supported by context and domain terminology.

   - < 0.50: Pure guess or unclassifiable text.
