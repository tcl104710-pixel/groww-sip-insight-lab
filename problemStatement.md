# SIP Insight Lab — AI-Powered Discovery Engine

## Problem Statement

> **Context:** Product Manager, Growth Team · Groww

---

## 🎯 Business Goal

Increase the percentage of **first-time mutual fund investors** who still have an **active SIP 12 months** after starting their first one.

> **Definition of "Active SIP":** At least one successful instalment processed in the last 30 days.

---

## 🔍 The Core Problem

First-time investors stop, pause, or lose their SIPs for **very different reasons**, each requiring a fundamentally different fix:

| Reason | Description |
|---|---|
| **Silent Lapse** | A debit fails (e.g., salary hasn't arrived); after three misses the SIP is auto-cancelled — no deliberate decision made |
| **UX / Payment Friction** | The app or payment flow confused the investor |
| **Return Disappointment** | Early returns missed what a calculator, friend, or finfluencer promised |
| **Income Change** | Personal financial circumstances shifted |
| **Informed Choice** | A deliberate, considered decision to stop |

The evidence is **scattered** across app reviews, Reddit, investing forums, and YouTube comments — with no consistent way to distinguish these categories or measure their frequency.

---

## ⚠️ Three Evidence Traps

### 1. Saying ≠ Doing
A user who writes *"I'm thinking of stopping"* or sounds negative has **not necessarily stopped**. Intent and action must be recorded **separately**.

### 2. Not Every Stop Is a Decision
An **involuntary lapse** and a **chosen stop** look identical in a retention metric, but call for **opposite solutions**.

### 3. Survivorship Bias
Studying only the investors who leave **hides half the answer**. What protects the investors who *stay* matters as much as what pushes others out.

---

## 🤖 The Solution: SIP Insight Lab

An **AI-powered discovery engine** that:

1. **Collects** permitted public feedback from:
   - Google Play Store
   - Apple App Store
   - Reddit
   - TradingQ&A
   - YouTube comments

2. **Cleans** data — removes personal details and standardises format.

3. **Classifies** each item against a **consistent research taxonomy**, recording:
   - Investor lifecycle stage at time of comment
   - What they **actually did** (separate from stated intent)
   - Type of stop (involuntary lapse vs. deliberate exit)
   - Root cause of churn or retention
   - Trigger moment (when in the first 12 months)
   - External help sources consulted (outside the app)
   - For continuers — **what kept them going**

---

## 💡 Key Insights Revealed

- The **gap between stated intent and actual action**
- How much churn comes from **silent, involuntary lapses**
- **When** in the first 12 months doubt or failure strikes
- What **separates continuers** from those who drop off
- How patterns differ by:
  - Age
  - Income stability
  - Source of money
  - Who influenced the investor (finfluencer, friend, etc.)

---

## 🔗 Evidence Integrity

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Source traceability** | Every insight links back to its original quotes and sources |
| **Counter-evidence agent** | A second AI agent actively challenges each leading theme by searching for contradicting evidence |
| **Hypothesis cards** | Strongest opportunities are packaged with interview guides for user-interview validation |

---

## ✅ Quality & Measurement

Quality is **measured, not assumed**:

- AI classifications compared against a **hand-labelled golden set** (minimum 20 examples)
- Agreement rates and errors are **reported openly**
- Low-confidence items routed to **human review**
- Every correction or rejection of AI output is logged in an exportable **AI Evidence Log**
- Opportunities ranked using a **visible formula** based on:
  - Frequency
  - Severity
  - Concentration in the **first six months**
  - Whether the problem can be solved without investment advice or fear-based nudges

---

## 🛡️ Ethics & Guardrails

Public feedback is treated as a source of **hypotheses**, not as a measure of retention or proof of causality.

- Findings will be validated through **user interviews** and, where available, **internal behavioural data**
- The solution will **never** recommend specific funds or stocks
- Will **not** rely on fear, FOMO, urgency, or manipulative nudges

---

## 🏁 Success Criteria

Replace scattered opinions with **traceable evidence** that shows Groww:

1. **Why** first-time investors stop
2. **Which** stops a better product could have prevented
